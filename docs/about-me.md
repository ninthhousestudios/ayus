My name is Josh Harper. I first met Amma in 2019 in Seattle. I was a driver for the
summer tour in 2024 and I enjoy visiting Chicago and Ann Arbor satsangs when I can. I
live in Indianapolis, so it isn't too far, but it also isn't too close.

When I was last at the Chicago ashram for the Spring Renewal retreat with Swamiji and
Swamini I heard about the Amrita Institute for Advanced Research. I remembered that I
was actually at the program in 2024 when it was announced, but I didn't really
understand what is was about. I was curious after hearing about it again so I looked on
the website to understand more about the Institute and what it is doing.

I have had many interests in life but somehow I ended up with a BS in Mathematics and a
minor in Computer Science. I graduated in 2012 and did not apply this degree in any way
after university ended. Then about two years ago I started studying astrology. At the
time I was not able to purchase the astrology software I wanted, so I started to write
my own by hand in Python. This was a great exercise and learning experience. I had
always been interested in programming but never had a real idea of what I wanted to
create. This provided the opportunity for me to get back into programming by writing
something I was actually motivated to write. For the last three months I have been
writing software in collaboration with Claude, including a number of astrology apps, for
desktop and mobile, as well as bespoke tools to help me in my workflows.

I was intrigued by the Agentic AI for Ayurveda Analytics that was mentioned on the
Projects page. I had been interested in having agents do astrology on some level. Of
course, Claude has much astrological knowledge in its training data, but that isn't
very reliable. This lead to the idea of Vidya, a structured knowledge graph. I
realized this might be applicable to Ayurveda as well and so I made this as a
demonstration of one potential way of working with these ideas.

Ayus was written in Rust, designed and directed by me in collaboration with Claude. I am
currently pursuing a deeper study of Rust and machine learning. I would love to
contribute to Amma's projects in some way. At the moment most of the conversation around
AI is about using it to make more money faster, but I think there are much better uses
to which we can put this technology.
