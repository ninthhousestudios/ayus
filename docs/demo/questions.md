# Vidya demo — questions

Ask each question verbatim. Run once with the vidya agent (system prompt + MCP tools), once with bare Claude (no system prompt, no tools). Capture both raw outputs.

---

1. According to Charaka, which of the six rasas (tastes) pacify Vata dosha, and which aggravate it?

2. According to Charaka, what are the three categories of vipaka (post-digestive effect), and which rasas map to each?

3. Does Charaka record any rule about consuming radish or garlic with milk? If so, what does he say?

4. Does Charaka warn against combining turmeric (haridra) with milk?

5. What food combinations involving honey does Charaka describe as viruddha (incompatible)?

6. What dosage of ashwagandha does Charaka recommend for daily use?

7. What does Charaka say about the properties of pippali (long pepper)? Cite each claim to its source verse.

8. For Charaka's statement that madhura (sweet) rasa is guru, snigdha, and sheeta — what is the exact source citation?

9. Compare haritaki and amalaki according to Charaka — where do their properties agree and where do they differ?
